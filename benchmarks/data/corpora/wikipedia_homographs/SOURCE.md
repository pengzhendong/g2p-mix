# Wikipedia Homograph Data provenance

`wordids.tsv`, `eval/*.tsv`, and `LICENSE` are copied unchanged from revision
`8f008f021e88f8b71118a27ae655f1f3121162bc` of
<https://github.com/google-research-datasets/WikipediaHomographData>.

Only the official eval split is bundled: 1,615 examples for 162 homographs.
The corpus is Apache-2.0 licensed. Its `wordid` annotation is the benchmark
gold. The accompanying IPA is used only to define distinct pronunciation
classes; it is not treated as an authoritative exact transcription.
